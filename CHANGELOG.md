# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First version: videos (adaptive HLS up to 1080p, or a 360p MP4), shorts with a swipe player, live streams, channels,
playlists, search with suggestions, comments, related videos, subscriptions, history and "watch later" kept on the
device (no account), SponsorBlock, captions, Return YouTube Dislike counts, quality and speed choice, background sound,
light and dark theme, English and Czech. Other apps can open content with the `tubie:` URL scheme (`watch/<id>`,
`short/<id>`, `channel/<id or @handle>`, `playlist/<id>`, `search?q=`, `open?url=<youtube link>`).
